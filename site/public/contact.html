Fios thugainn — get in touch

For a school, a teacher or a Gaelic-education contact wanting to hear more. This form asks for three things only: your school (or organisation), your role, and an email address. Nothing about any pupil, and nothing is required beyond this.

This form does not go anywhere yet. It posts to https://forms.scotling.example/placeholder-expression-of-interest, a clearly marked placeholder endpoint — nobody receives this submission today. Wiring it to a real inbox is a Founder Card "prepared, not executed" item (Constitution 7.7.3): it is an external action and needs a founder yes/no first, same as any other live contact point. Until then, if you are reading this as a real person: email the founder directly instead of using this form.

We collect only what is above. No pupil names, no pupil data, no payment details, no account creation. See Privacy for the full position.
