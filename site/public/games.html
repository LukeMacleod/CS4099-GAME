Geamannan

The games

Scotling Play, ages 4–16, is the current focus of the whole Scotling ecosystem (alongside the planned Scotling Schools, Scotling Home and Scotling Community).

Full page draft pending the Children's Game Designer, Game Frontend Engineer and Visual Brand Designer pods — this is an internal placeholder with only confirmed facts, so the site navigates without a dead link. Please revisit once their content lands.

What exists today

A working build (cs4099-game.web.app) with roughly 12 minutes of games, real recorded narration, and a shared class code (P-7) rather than individual pupil logins. There is no save data and no progress tracking in the current build. 26 unique Gaelic words and 38 voiced lines are in it today.

Where it's going

The founder's direction for v2 is more game variety, more cognitive range, more culture and tradition woven into the mechanics themselves — not just the surface text — and craft that aims much higher visually. Sizing that ambition against a solo founder's real time budget is ongoing work for the Product pod and the Board; nothing above is a promise of a ship date.

See Evidence for what was actually tested, and For schools for how a class can try it.
