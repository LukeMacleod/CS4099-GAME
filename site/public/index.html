What Scotling is

A small, Gaelic-medium games product (Scotling Play, ages 4–16) sitting inside a wider plan: Scotling Play, Scotling Schools, Scotling Home and Scotling Community. One person builds it, full-time-job-and-all, using AI tooling as a force multiplier — every recommendation this studio makes has to be something a solo founder can actually execute.
