For parents and grandparents

Teaghlaichean — for families

Scotling Home is the planned family-facing part of the Scotling ecosystem. It does not exist as a product yet — this page states only what one classroom trial found, because that is the honest reason this part of the plan exists at all.

Full page draft pending the UX Research Lead and Narrative/Character Designer pods — this is an internal placeholder so the site navigates without a dead link.

What children told us, unprompted

In the one classroom trial run so far (18 P6–P7 pupils), several children — without being asked — described an uneven Gaelic relationship with their grandparents, and suggested playing the game together could help. Two children said they wanted to use it to teach Gaelic to parents who do not speak it. This is a genuinely small, single-classroom finding, not a promise about what any one family will experience — see Evidence for the full, honest picture, including where that evidence runs out.

What exists for a family today

Nothing bought or installed separately from a school — Scotling Play today is deployed for classroom use, with no family-facing login or subscription yet.
